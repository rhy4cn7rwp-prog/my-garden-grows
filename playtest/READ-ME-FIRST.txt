MY GARDEN GROWS 1.0 — DEVELOPMENT PLAYTEST

No Terminal, installation, or account inside the game is needed.

1. If you downloaded a ZIP, double-click it in Finder to unzip it.
2. Open the playtest folder.
3. Right-click My-Garden-Grows-1.0.html and choose Open With > Google Chrome
   (or Safari).

Keep using the same file and browser to retain your farm progress.
Private browsing or browser storage restrictions may prevent progress saving.
This is a development copy. Playing it does not change the live website.
